Meeting Rooms

At The Paddocks Hotel, we can provide the ideal private space for your meeting, workshop or conference. We have a range of private spaces available for hire depending on your capacity needs, with our largest function room seating up to 200 people. 

Refreshments can be provided according to your needs, with packages tailored to your company’s individual requirements. To make your event as stress-free as possible, the hotel offers plenty of free, convenient parking.
